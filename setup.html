NudgeSheet Setup Guide

NudgeSheet works with a Google Sheet you already use to track leads. There is no new template to adopt — you map your existing columns once, and NudgeSheet reads them from then on.

1. Install and open

  1. Install NudgeSheet from the Google Workspace Marketplace into the Google account that owns (or can edit) your lead spreadsheet.
  2. Open the spreadsheet you want to track. A NudgeSheet menu appears next to Extensions.
  3. The first time you open the add-on, Google will ask you to review and approve the permissions it needs. See the Privacy Policy for exactly what each permission is used for.

2. Map your columns

  1. From the NudgeSheet menu, choose Configure Columns.
  2. Match your sheet's existing header row to NudgeSheet's fields:
    • Required: Lead Name, Next Follow-up Date
    • Optional: Company, Email, Status, Last Contact Date, Notes
  3. Save. NudgeSheet remembers this mapping for this specific sheet.

3. Use the follow-up inbox

  1. From the NudgeSheet menu, choose Open Inbox to open the sidebar.
  2. Leads are grouped into OVERDUE, TODAY, and UPCOMING (next 7 days), sorted by date.
  3. After you reach out to someone, click Done to set their next follow-up date (or end follow-up), or Snooze to push the date back without marking it done.
  4. Click Refresh any time you've edited the sheet directly and want the inbox to catch up.

4. Turn on the daily reminder (optional)

  1. From the NudgeSheet menu, choose Daily Reminder Settings.
  2. Turn reminders on and pick an hour, in your spreadsheet's own time zone.
  3. You'll get at most one email a day, only on days you have at least one overdue or due-today follow-up, sent only to your own Google account.

Uninstalling

Remove NudgeSheet from Extensions → Add-ons → Manage add-ons, or from the Google Workspace Marketplace. Your lead data stays in your spreadsheet exactly as it is; NudgeSheet never stored a separate copy of it.
